Zonneveld Engineering

From the port to offshore execution.

Zonneveld Engineering is a small engineering company based in Germany, working on offshore wind. Being small means close attention: the person who prepares the work is the person who follows it through to execution.

Experience

In port

Port operations and load-out

Management of a marshalling port on Denmark’s west coast: receipt, storage and load-out of components, coordination of contractors and vessel interfaces.

On board

Field engineering and installation

Engineering support for the offshore installation of foundations and secondary steel from an installation vessel: lift preparation, technical troubleshooting and changes to installation methods.

In the project office

Project engineering and coordination

Bringing together clients, vessel crews and specialist subcontractors through technical documentation, proposal preparation and coordination of operational requirements.

Behind the company

Daniel Zonneveld, founder and managing director. A Brazilian engineer based in Germany, with international experience in offshore wind, energy and infrastructure. Degree in Environmental Engineering, with postgraduate studies in Railway Engineering and Occupational Safety Engineering.
