Services

How we can support your project.

Offshore installation

Practical engineering support from preparation to offshore execution.

  • Installation methods and operational readiness reviews.
  • Support for secondary steel installation and equipment troubleshooting.
  • Technical coordination between vessel, client and specialist teams.

Lifting & operational change

Clear procedures for lifting operations and changing site conditions.

  • Preparation and review of lift plans, rigging arrangements and procedures.
  • Risk assessments and method statements (RAMS).
  • Management of Change (MoC) documentation and revised work sequences.

Port & load-out operations

Coordination of components, contractors and vessel interfaces.

  • Planning and coordination of load-in, storage and load-out.
  • Component condition inspections and readiness follow-up.
  • Tracking movements, vessel laydays and mobilisation activities.

Project & interface coordination

Keeping technical requirements connected to daily delivery.

  • Interface management across clients, suppliers and contractors.
  • Schedule follow-up, progress reporting and action tracking.
  • Support for site teams, project documentation and handover.

Transport & installation studies

Operational input for early decisions and tender preparation.

  • Comparison of vessel deployment and installation concepts.
  • Analysis of campaign progress, constraints and operational costs.
  • Technical and commercial input to T&I tender documentation.

Documentation & project tools

Structured information that helps teams plan and follow the work.

  • Practical reporting formats and document workflows.
  • Support for digital project and document management tools.
  • Consolidation of operational records, lessons learned and indicators.

Support tailored to a defined project scope, from individual technical tasks to coordination during execution.
